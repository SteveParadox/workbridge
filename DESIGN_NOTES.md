# WorkBridge Design Notes

The visual direction is based on staffing and HR consulting patterns from related firms, adapted for WorkBridge Consulting NG rather than copied directly.

Reference takeaways:

- Nigerian HR/outsourcing firms emphasize institutional trust, local market credibility, and business-service clarity.
- Larger staffing brands separate employer and job-seeker paths early, keep role categories easy to scan, and use clear process steps.
- WorkBridge should feel more focused and personal than a broad enterprise HR firm, so the site uses concise copy, direct CTAs, WhatsApp access, and practical role categories.

Design choices:

- Navy and white lead the interface; gold is limited to small status, icon, and divider accents.
- Forms are prominent because the primary conversion path is business owner to request staff.
- The services section uses a mixed editorial grid so priority operational roles stand out.
- The process timeline makes staffing feel structured and lower-risk.
- No invented statistics, testimonials, awards, or partner logos are used.
